Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is The 33rd Executive Suites?

We are on Babesa Highway, Thimphu 11001, Bhutan, in Babesa on the southern side of Thimphu, Bhutan’s capital — on the road in from Paro International Airport.

Is breakfast included?

Every room can be booked with or without breakfast. With breakfast, guests enjoy the daily vegetarian breakfast buffet at Artisan’s Table; without breakfast, the rate is for the room only. You choose when you send your booking request.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made less than 45 days before the scheduled arrival date are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. Cancellations made 30 days or less before arrival, and no-shows, are charged the full amount of the reservation. Group bookings of more than 5 rooms have different cancellation policies — please contact reservations for the details.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM. Late check-out can be requested when you book, subject to availability.

Is the hotel vegetarian?

Yes. The 33rd Executive Suites is one of Bhutan’s only 100% vegetarian and vegan-friendly hotels. Breakfast, the Artisan’s Table restaurant, Private Dining, Salts Rooftop Bar and Sugar Box Café all serve vegetarian food.

Do you offer airport transfers from Paro?

Yes. A private chauffeured transfer between Paro International Airport and the hotel can be added when you book.

What is included in every room?

Rooms have a rain shower, high-speed Wi-Fi, a smart TV, a minibar, tea and coffee facilities and 24-hour room service, and most have a mini kitchen and microwave. The Valley and Executive Suites also have a bathtub. Every room can be booked with or without our daily vegetarian breakfast, and fitness centre access is included in your stay.

How do I book and see the rates?

Choose your dates and room on our website and send a booking request — our reservations team replies with availability and the best available rate. You can also call or WhatsApp +975 16993333 or email reservation@the33rdes.com.

Does the hotel have a spa and a rooftop bar?

Yes. Om Spa offers massages with steam and sauna, and Salts Rooftop Bar on the seventh floor is open daily from 10:00 AM to midnight with views over the valley.
