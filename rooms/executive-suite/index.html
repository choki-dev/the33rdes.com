Before you arrive

Guest guidelines

Please review the following guidelines to ensure a safe and enjoyable stay.

Early arrival / late departure

  • Early arrival: check-in is at 2:00 PM. Early arrivals can store luggage and use our facilities until the room is ready. Early check-in is subject to availability and may incur an extra fee.
  • Late departure: check-out is at 12:00 PM. Late check-out requests are subject to availability and additional charges. For a guaranteed late check-out, please contact reception in advance.

Cancellation policy

  • Cancellations made less than 45 days before the scheduled arrival date will be subject to a 50% cancellation fee.
  • Cancellations made 30 days or less before the scheduled arrival date, as well as no-shows, will be charged the full amount of the reservation.
  • Group bookings (more than 5 rooms) have different cancellation policies; please contact reservations for the details.
