Welcome to The 33rd Executive Suites

Thoughtful Living. Conscious Comfort. Quiet Elegance.

Nestled in the serene city of Thimphu, The 33rd Executive Suites is a 4-star hotel where contemporary comfort meets conscious living. Designed for discerning travelers, we offer a refined and welcoming experience rooted in wellness, sustainability, and mindful hospitality.

As one of Bhutan’s few hotels dedicated to a 100% vegetarian and vegan-friendly experience, we believe that exceptional hospitality goes beyond comfort, it reflects a deeper respect for people, the planet, and the choices we make.

With personalized service, international standards of care, and an atmosphere of understated elegance, every stay is thoughtfully crafted to help you feel at ease.

More than a place to stay, The 33rd is a place to reconnect with yourself, your values, and the journey that brings you here.
